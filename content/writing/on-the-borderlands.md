Some of my clearest thinking has happened at edges — between languages, between
disciplines, between who I was and who I was becoming. This is a short reflection
that grew out of teaching [[teaching/nepantla-liminal-spaces|Nepantla]].

The border is not only a line on a map. It is a condition: a place of friction that
asks something of us. To write from the borderlands is to refuse the comfort of a
single, settled position.
