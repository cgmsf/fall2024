> A handout for our unit on borderlands thought.

**Nepantla**, from the Nahuatl word for "in the middle," names the in-between —
the disorienting, generative space we occupy during transition. Gloria Anzaldúa
took up the term to describe the threshold where old certainties loosen and new
understandings become possible.

## Key ideas

- Nepantla is uncomfortable *and* productive.
- Liminal spaces are where identity is renegotiated.
- Knowledge made "in the middle" is often the most honest.

## Discussion questions

1. Describe a time you were "in the middle." What did it make possible?
2. How does Anzaldúa reframe discomfort as a site of learning?
